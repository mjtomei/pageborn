Library 0

Add Markdown, plain text or PDF files from your phone. Your library is saved on this device. Tap a document to read it on your glasses, then use the glasses or the controls in the app to navigate.

Keep reading while files are being added. Open any file once it's added.

Sort by recently opened, newest added, or file name. The glasses document picker uses the same choice.

Use the replay button beside Library to sync all PDFs, Markdown and text files with the shared desktop folder through your Even Terminal connection. In the glasses document picker, hold to sync. Select Librarian for a conversation across the shared library.

Additions, edits and deletions transfer both ways. Conflicting edits keep both copies; previous synced versions remain in the desktop folder’s hidden .pageborne/history directory. Ask uses this same library. Stock Terminal needs the optional Pageborne library extension.

    Voice inputOn device

    Names and technical terms, separated by commas or new lines. Shared across documents and prioritized over automatic terms. Each model uses as many as it can fit. These guide local transcription; they do not force a spelling.

    Custom terms come first, followed by words from your reading position and recent conversation. Hints encourage a spelling; they do not force it.

    Last transcription

    Local dictation shows provisional words while you speak. Stop speaking to edit the final draft or send it.

    Conformer Small, Zipformer 20M and Moonshine Base run together on the CPU. First-open setup downloads about 249 MB once, then checks and keeps the models ready until exit. Verified downloads are reused across app updates while saved app data is retained. Audio stays on this device; the recognized question goes to your agent. Gray words have combined confidence below 0.70; they may still be correct. The glasses dim the newest uncertain words that fit; the phone marks all of them.

    Formatting

    Saved for this PDF only. Changes rebuild the preview and keep your place; the original file isn't changed.

    Reflow body text: Prose uses the glasses’ native text; figures, tables and equations stay images. Scans use on-device OCR, which can be slow and may contain errors.

    Fit figures to one screen: Very tall figures stay scrollable. Turn off for more detail in dense figures.

    Keep margin notes: Side notes and stamps. Only those lines shrink to fit.

    Keep headers & page numbers: off hides page numbers, running headers and footers, and download watermarks.

    Normalize PDF colors: Dark ink on light paper here, bright ink on the glasses.

    Defaults for new PDFs

    Defaults apply the first time you open a PDF. Opened PDFs keep their own formatting.
